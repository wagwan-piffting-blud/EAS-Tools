# WarnGen comparison fields

Compare in this order so structural failures are not hidden by prose differences.

1. Product family, era, office, and action.
2. WMO heading, issuing office, PIL, and product class.
3. VTEC class, action, office, phenomena, significance, event tracking number, start, and end.
4. UGC type, ordering, continuation lines, purge time, and selected/cancelled areas.
5. Issue, effective, expiration, and follow-up timing.
6. Bullet selection and XML defaults.
7. Immediate cause, source, impact, hazard, call to action, and emergency wording.
8. Location, pathcast, portion-of-area, county/zone/marine wording, and office-specific phrasing.
9. Line wrapping, separators, terminators, and trailing whitespace only after semantic fields match.

For every mismatch record: reference value, rendered value, responsible layer if established, evidence, and classification. Do not label normal office or era variance as a defect without an accepted parity target.
