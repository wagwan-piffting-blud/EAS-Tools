EAS Tools - TTS Voice List

Text-to-Speech (TTS) Voice List

This is a simple list of where to find every single TTS voice used on the Web TTS service and within EAS Tools as WASM voices. Some voices may require activation/a license, but most are available for free usage. If you have a voice on the "Wanted" list, let me know, and I will add it to the TTS service if you have a copy of the actual software!

THE WANTED LIST

This is a list of TTS voices that are wanted for, but are not currently available in, the EAS Tools Web TTS service for one reason or another. Some of these are lost media, some are proprietary, some are just... not easily found on the open Internet as it stands in the present day, or are prohibitively expensive to obtain (looking at you, Google WaveNet...). The list is non-exhaustive and incomplete, but it serves as a starting point for those interested in expanding the TTS service. List is ordered by priority and previous usage within the Emergency Alert System.

  1. Speechify Mara. This is the biggest voice we're missing today, due to its limited distribution and mostly business-to-business use. You may recognize this voice as NWS "Donna". Notably, it is derived from, but not the same as, AT&T NaturalVoices Crystal. I have a whole entire page dedicated to "The Hunt for Speechify Mara", check it out on my personal website. This is a high-value target with a cash bounty being offered if found.
  2. Speechify Rick. Similar to Mara, Rick is a high-value voice that is currently unavailable. Not much is known about Rick (not even what he might sound like), but it may be the derivation of AT&T NaturalVoices Mike, paired with Mara/Crystal.
  3. IBM Allison, V2, expressive/non-expressive. We have V1 and V3/V3 Expressive (both V3's neural) available today in the TTS service, but V2 is still missing, and was most commonly used around 2015-2020 (?, dates approximate) for Alert Ready alerts in Canada.
  4. Google WaveNet, Voice E. This is a neural voice using Google DeepMind's "WaveNet" technology. It is known for its high-quality speech synthesis and is often used specifically for Arizona Department of Public Safety "SAFE Alerts". If there happens to be a locally runnable copy of this voice, it would be a valuable addition to the TTS service, so I don't have to wrap Google's (expensive, proprietary) API calls.

THE AVAILABLE LIST

This is the list of TTS voices that ARE currently available in the EAS Tools Web TTS service. These voices are all obtainable in some way, shape, or form, from some source or another, and are either free to use, or require a license key/some type of payment for usage. It is solely your responsibility to ensure you are using these voices legally according to their respective licenses and distribution terms. I do not, nor can I advocate for, piracy/bypassing of licensing restrictions by any means. Each voice comes with a link to download a copy for your local use, but some may have "nag" audio, or other limitations associated with them. Some voices are grouped by their engine, this usually means you can have all of the voices downloaded alongside the engine, therefore, only one download link is necessary. Voices are listed in the same order they are in within the TTS service's JSON voice list.
